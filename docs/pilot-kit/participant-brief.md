# Participant brief

This is a feasibility exercise of a local data-onboarding tool. It is not a test of the participant,
a production rollout, or a promise of faster work. The tool proposes mappings and generates tested
artifacts; an authorized, separate reviewer must make approval and certification decisions.

The operator receives a frozen input and task definition, completes a short unrelated training
exercise, then performs the agreed manual and/or assisted tasks. The reviewer receives expected
business definitions prepared before seeing assisted proposals and checks the agreed outputs.
Both participants record time and difficulties; any developer help is logged.

The coordinator agrees the time cap, session schedule and output scope with participants before
starting. A profiling-only exercise is a valid smaller task; it must not be reported as a complete
certified financial workflow. The operator can stop and report inability to complete a task.

Use opaque actor identifiers in measurement records. Store data, names, authorization documents
and detailed observations privately. Permission to participate does not automatically permit
publication, quotations, company identification, or sharing metadata with a model provider.
Keep model calls disabled unless the exact disclosure has been separately authorized.

The operator confirms the time/assistance log. The reviewer signs their assessment of correctness.
The data owner decides what can be shared and when private records and backups must be deleted.
These are separate decisions. Explain how to withdraw before publication and follow the agreed
retention/deletion process. Leave unknown values blank/null rather than filling in favorable values.

At the end, the report should describe what was attempted, whether it worked, how much setup and
review it needed, what failed and how much assistance was required. One participant's experience
does not establish enterprise readiness, typical savings, or broad schema accuracy.
